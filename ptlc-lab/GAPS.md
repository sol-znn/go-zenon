| G-17 | **The contract checks an Ed25519 lock for its length only.** Thirty-two bytes that encode no point make an entry no signature opens; a point of small order makes one that signatures anybody can write down open, because the contract verifies with Go's `crypto/ed25519`, which takes such a key. Refused by the console (`lockProblem`) and the CLI (`ptlc.create`) since 2026-10-09; shown by the page's `weak-key`. The alternative the owner turned down: decode the key at `Create` and in `checkStoredPtlcInfo`, and refuse a non-canonical encoding and a point whose eightfold is the identity, as libsodium and Oasis Core do | accepted · 2026-10-09 |
